# Brand

Everything ESGCounts-branded (site, dashboard, emails, generated artifacts, docs, decks) must follow
`brand/BRAND.md` and use the logo files in `public/brand/`. Never redraw the logo or use off-brand colours.

# Reporting formats

Digital reporting requirements (CSRD / ESEF / Inline XBRL) are in `docs/specs/XBRL.md`. Report exports
must stay human- and machine-readable; keep the Inline XBRL and Excel exports in sync with `lib/gri/catalog.ts`.
